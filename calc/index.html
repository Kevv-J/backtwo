What Champions changes

Stat Points, not EVs

Champions replaces EVs with Stat Points: 66 total, at most 32 in any one stat. 32 SP is a fully invested stat, equivalent to the old 252 EVs. Every Pokémon is Level 50 with maximum IVs, so a spread is purely how you divide those 66 points.

Mega Evolution is back

Megas are legal, and only one can be active per battle even if you bring several stones. The calculator applies the Mega's ability and stats, and lets you toggle back to the base form without losing the ability from your paste.

No Terastallization

There is no Tera mechanic in Champions, so defensive typing is fixed and a resist stays a resist. Status is limited to burn, freeze, paralysis, sleep, poison and toxic.

Doubles by default

Spread moves take the 0.75× reduction, and Intimidate, Friend Guard, weather, terrain, Trick Room, Gravity and Fairy Aura are all modelled as field state you can toggle.

Champions-exclusive Mega abilities

Several Megas in Champions carry abilities that do not exist elsewhere. These are implemented in the calculator.

Calc against what people actually run

Spreads are only useful against real sets. Pull an opposing Pokémon's common item, ability and moves from its usage page, or find a whole team to test against with the team finder.
